# What is Networking?

**Computer networking** is the process of connecting computers, mobile devices, servers, and other devices so they can communicate and share data and resources.

### Types of networks
1. **PAN (Personal Area Network):** Covers a very small area around a person.
2. **LAN (Local Area Network):** Connects devices in a limited area such as a home, office, or college.
3. **MAN (Metropolitan Area Network):** Covers a city or large campus area.
4. **WAN (Wide Area Network):** Covers large geographical areas. The Internet is the largest example.

### Basic networking devices
- Router
- Switch
- Modem
- Wireless access point
- Network Interface Card (NIC)

### Benefits
- Resource sharing
- File and data sharing
- Communication
- Internet access
- Centralized services

Networking allows connected devices to exchange information using agreed communication protocols.
