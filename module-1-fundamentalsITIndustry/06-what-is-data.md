# What is Data?

**Data** is a collection of raw facts, values, observations, or symbols that can be processed to obtain useful information.

### Examples
- Name: Kavish
- Age: 20
- Temperature: 32.5°C
- Order amount: ₹850
- Photograph or video
- Customer feedback

### Types of data
**1. Structured data**  
Organized in a fixed format, such as rows and columns in a database.

**2. Semi-structured data**  
Has some organization but does not follow a rigid table structure, such as JSON or XML.

**3. Unstructured data**  
Does not have a predefined tabular structure, such as images, audio, and videos.

### Data vs Information
**Data** is the raw input.  
**Information** is meaningful knowledge obtained after processing and interpreting data.

### Example
Raw sales values:
`500, 700, 800, 600`

After calculating their average, the result becomes useful **information** about average sales.
