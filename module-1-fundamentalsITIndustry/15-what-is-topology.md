# What is Network Topology?

**Network topology** describes the physical or logical arrangement of devices and connections in a computer network.

### Common types

**1. Bus topology**  
All devices share a common communication cable. It is simple but a failure in the main cable can affect the network.

**2. Star topology**  
All devices connect to a central switch or hub. It is commonly used in modern LANs.

**3. Ring topology**  
Devices are connected in a closed loop.

**4. Mesh topology**  
Devices have multiple connections with each other, providing redundancy and fault tolerance.

**5. Tree topology**  
Devices are arranged in a hierarchical structure.

**6. Hybrid topology**  
Combines two or more different topology types.

### Importance
Topology affects network performance, scalability, cost, maintenance, and fault tolerance.
