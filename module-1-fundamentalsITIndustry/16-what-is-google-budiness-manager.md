# What is Google Business Profile?

**Google Business Profile** is a free Google service that allows eligible businesses and organizations to manage their presence on Google Search and Google Maps.

### Information that can be managed
- Business name
- Address or service area
- Phone number
- Website
- Opening hours
- Photos
- Products or services
- Customer reviews

### Benefits
- Helps customers find a business in local search.
- Provides important business information.
- Allows businesses to publish updates and photos.
- Allows businesses to respond to customer reviews.
- Provides performance insights about how customers discover the profile.

### Example
A restaurant can use a Google Business Profile to display its location, opening hours, phone number, photos, menu information, and reviews.

> **Note:** Google Business Profile was previously known as Google My Business.
