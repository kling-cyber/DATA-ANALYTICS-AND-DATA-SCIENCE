# What is SEO?

**SEO (Search Engine Optimization)** is the process of improving a website so that search engines can better discover, understand, and present its pages for relevant searches.

### Main areas of SEO

**1. On-page SEO**
- Useful and relevant content
- Descriptive page titles
- Clear headings
- Good internal linking
- Relevant keywords used naturally
- Descriptive image alt text

**2. Technical SEO**
- Mobile-friendly pages
- Fast loading performance
- Crawlable website structure
- HTTPS security
- Proper redirects and canonical URLs
- Structured data where appropriate

**3. Off-page SEO**
- Earning relevant links from other websites
- Building a website's reputation and visibility

### Example
A restaurant website can provide useful pages about its menu, location, opening hours, and services so search engines can understand the business and users can find relevant information.

SEO focuses on making website content **useful, understandable, accessible, and discoverable**.
