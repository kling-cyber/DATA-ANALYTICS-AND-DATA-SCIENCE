# What is Internet?

The **Internet** is a worldwide network of interconnected computers and devices that communicate and exchange information using standardized networking protocols, especially the TCP/IP protocol suite.

### How the Internet Works
1. A device connects to the Internet through an Internet Service Provider (ISP) or another network.
2. Data is divided into packets.
3. Routers forward the packets through interconnected networks.
4. Protocols such as TCP/IP help deliver the data to its destination.
5. The receiving device reassembles and processes the data.

### Common Uses
- Web browsing
- Email
- Online communication
- Cloud services
- File sharing
- Online education and entertainment

### Internet vs WWW
The **Internet** is the underlying global network infrastructure. The **World Wide Web** is one service that operates on the Internet.
